Thu–Sat Lifting Plan

Three gym days in a row. Each day trains different muscles, so you can train back to back and recover Sunday to Wednesday.

  1. SunWalk
  2. MonWalk
  3. TueWalk
  4. WedWalk
  5. ThuPush
  6. FriLegs
  7. SatPull

Every session

Warm up
5 min easy on the bike, then 1–2 light sets of the first exercise.
Effort
End each set with 1–2 good reps still in you.
Tempo
Lift in about 1 second, lower in about 2.
Progress
Hit the top of the rep range on every set, then add 2.5–5 kg the next week.

Main muscleHelping

Side view

ChestTriceps

Side view

Upper chestTriceps

  • Handles start level with your upper chest.

Shoulder press

3 × 8–10
Side view

ShouldersTriceps

  • Start with the handles at chin height and your back flat on the pad.
  • Press straight up and stop just before your elbows lock.
4

Cable lateral raise

4 × 12–15
Front view

Side shoulders

  • Set the pulley at the bottom, stand side-on to it and hold the handle with your far hand.
  • Lift your arm out to the side to shoulder height, lower slowly, then do the same reps with the other arm.
5

Triceps pushdown

3 × 10–12
Side view

Triceps

  • Keep your elbows pinned to your sides the whole time.
  • Push down until your arms are straight, then let your hands come back up to chest height.

How to do each one

Main muscleHelping

1

Leg press

4 × 8–10
Side view

QuadsGlutes

  • Feet shoulder-width apart in the middle of the platform.
  • Lower until your knees reach about 90°. Keep your lower back on the pad and don't lock your knees at the top.
2

Seated leg curl

3 × 10–12
Side view

HamstringsCalves

  • Line your knees up with the pivot. The roller sits just above your heels.
  • Curl as far as you can, pause, then let it rise slowly.
3

Leg extension

3 × 10–12
Side view

Quads

  • Knees in line with the pivot, pad on your lower shins.
  • Straighten your legs fully and hold for 1 second at the top.
4

Hip thrust machine

3 × 8–12
Side view

GlutesHamstrings

  • Upper back on the pad, feet flat, shins vertical at the top.
  • Push through your heels until your body is straight from shoulders to knees, then squeeze.
5

Standing calf raise

4 × 10–15
Side view

Calves

  • Balls of your feet on the edge, heels hanging off.
  • Drop your heels for a stretch, then rise as high as you can and hold 1 second.
6

Ab crunch machine

3 × 10–15
Side view

Abs

  • Sit tall and hold the handles lightly.
  • Curl your chest toward your hips using your abs, not your arms. Breathe out as you crunch.
